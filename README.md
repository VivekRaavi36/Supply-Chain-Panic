# Supply Chain Panic

A warehouse management arcade game. Unload inbound trucks, store stock on limited shelves, and fill customer orders before their timers run out, while port strikes, short shipments, heatwaves and audits keep getting in the way.

It is a single self-contained `index.html` with no build step and no dependencies. Open it in a browser or host it on any static site host.

## How it maps to supply chain management

- **Inbound:** trucks are supplier deliveries; unloading a crate is a goods receipt and costs cash.
- **Storage:** limited slots, shelf life on produce and dairy, spoilage and audits.
- **Outbound:** orders with deadlines and service tiers; late orders cost reputation.
- **Report card:** service level, inventory turns, stockouts and spoilage.
